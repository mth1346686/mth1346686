## 🔬 About Me

I operate at the rare intersection of **clinical bedside medicine** and **advanced software/data engineering**. 

As an **Assistant Lecturer of Anesthesia & Surgical Intensive Care** at Tanta University holding an **MSc in Anesthesia and ICU**, I manage critical patient hemodynamics, surgical anesthesia, and ICU telemetry. Simultaneously, I architect scalable cloud data warehouses, train machine learning clinical deterioration models, and engineer responsive web and cross-platform mobile applications.

* 🩺 **Clinical Faculty:** Assistant Lecturer at **Faculty of Medicine, Tanta University** (MBBCh 2020, MSc, active **EDAIC** candidate). Doctoral research focused on neuro-anesthesia metrics and clinical data modeling.
* ☁️ **Cloud Data Engineer:** Fellow at **Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative (DEPI) - MCIT** on the **Microsoft Data Engineer** track (Azure Data Factory, Synapse, SQL).
* 🧠 **Neuro-Engineering & BCI:** Developing pipelines to ingest and process biological/neurological signals (EEG) across 10+ frequency bands for Brain-Computer Interface (BCI) research.
* 🤖 **Clinical Machine Learning:** Built ICU risk prediction models achieving **88% ROC-AUC** in early physiological deterioration detection.
* 📱 **Software & Mobile Engineering:** Building full-stack web platforms (**Next.js, React, Node.js**) and cross-platform mobile apps (**Flutter & Dart**).
* 🎓 **Global Programs:** Certified by **The University of Tokyo / Matsuo Lab** (GCI AI Program), **WorldQuant University** (Applied Data Science Lab), **DeepLearning.AI**, and **London Innovation Academy** (CPD Approved).
